Privacy Policy for Block Blast Pro

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

At Block Blast Pro (accessible from https://blockblastpro.firstlovestudios.com/), the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information that is received and collected by Block Blast Pro and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

Block Blast Pro does not collect personally identifiable information directly (such as names, emails, or phone numbers) unless you choose to provide it. Game state and high scores are saved locally on your device via HTML5 Local Storage.

We use third-party advertising companies, such as Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other sites on the Internet.

  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Log Files

Like many web sites, Block Blast Pro makes use of standard server log files. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, type of browser, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks to analyze trends and administer the site.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California (CCPA), you have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, rectify, or request erasure of your data collected by third-party services. To exercise these rights, please adjust your browser cookie settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@firstlovestudios.com.
